## Scale correction

User correctly flagged raw dispersion/error comparisons as scale-confounded. `scale_diagnostic.json` recomputes the fixed mean-corrected interpolation baseline from each example's known hourly means. March14: interpolation MAE3.00145, model/interpolation1.09231. April26: interpolation MAE9.71223, ratio1.03130. Both illustrated examples underperform interpolation. Thus raw narrow/wide MAE differences do not establish uncertainty discrimination beyond scale. Width divided by known hourly-curve standard deviation is0.49928 versus0.78711: the raw15.66x width contrast shrinks to1.58x. This is an illustrative diagnostic, not a validated normalization rule; flat hourly curves require special handling, and daily curve variability is not quarter reconstruction difficulty. Realized interpolation error is retrospective and unavailable for inference-time selection. Do not claim the wide case is merely scale or that a scale-adjusted uncertainty/error association has been established.
